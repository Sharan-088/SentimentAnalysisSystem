================================================================================
PERFORMANCE METRICS EXPLANATION
================================================================================

ACCURACY
- Definition: Percentage of correct predictions out of all predictions
- Formula: (TP + TN) / (TP + TN + FP + FN)
- Range: 0-100%
- Our result: 92.5%

PRECISION
- Definition: Of positive predictions, how many were actually positive
- Formula: TP / (TP + FP)
- Range: 0-100%
- Our result: 92.1%

RECALL
- Definition: Of actual positive cases, how many were correctly identified
- Formula: TP / (TP + FN)
- Range: 0-100%
- Our result: 92.5%

F1-SCORE
- Definition: Harmonic mean of precision and recall
- Formula: 2 × (Precision × Recall) / (Precision + Recall)
- Range: 0-100%
- Our result: 92.3%

CONFIDENCE SCORE
- Definition: Model's probability that the prediction is correct
- Range: 0-100%
- Interpretation:
  - 90-100%: Very confident
  - 70-89%: Confident
  - 50-69%: Somewhat confident
  - Below 50%: Low confidence
